## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-18

### Added
- `talk-studio` skill: a stateful talk workspace (`BRIEF.md`, `SOURCES.md`, `research/`, `FACTS.md`, `DECISIONS.md`, `OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`, `FLOW.md`, `LOG.md`) with a format file for each.
- Modes inferred from the conversation: intake, research, ask (questions answered from the knowledge base with sources and confidence, never silently from memory), discuss and decide, shape (skeleton first, then `FLOW.md`), review, optional slides.
- Stakes levels (casual, standard, high-stakes) that set research depth, the evidence bar on stage and extra artefacts.
- Narrative principles for the flow document: one story opens and closes, the thesis is said once near the end, one rhythm per section, few numbers, background for every name, data on the slides, designed conversation, fair framing, planned cuts.
- Evidence grades (strong, medium, weak, contested) and confidentiality rules for internal material.
